Zusammenfassung

Hook rules, caption/story/newsletter formats, posting rhythm, claims boundaries, and what performs on this account

Details

Hooks

- Hook quality is the highest-leverage lever in this content system

- Hooks must use the audience's own vocabulary, not Aisun's concept language

- Every hook must contain a concrete scene, friction/taboo-break, instant recognition, and an open loop

- Always generate 8–10 hook options per post and filter through the four-point check

- The first caption line is a standalone hook of maximum five words (the scroll-stopper visible before expanding)

- Hooks müssen absolut konkret und verständlich sein, möglichst mit Zahlen/konkreten Angaben (z. B. Prozente, konkrete Fälle) und einem Open Loop; „X Signale, die du übersiehst“-Listen-Hooks findet sie oldschool und will sie nicht mehr

- Reels: nie wieder B-Roll-Anweisungen liefern (macht sie selbst) — nur Hook und Caption

Content DNA (from catalog analysis)

- Top-performing formats: anti-doctor / friction-with-conventional-medicine provocation (highest reach by far); behind-the-scenes with family; concrete organ/symptom plus energetic interpretation; sexual healing / foreign energy

- Pure business/income content performs poorly on this account

Formats

- Captions always begin with an emoji, and nearly every sentence or short paragraph opens with an emoji

- Instagram-Captions: zwischen den Sätzen immer wieder Leerzeilen lassen (luftig, nicht als Block)

- Instagram-Captions enden immer mit drei passenden Keywords in GROSSBUCHSTABEN; INNERWISE ist immer dabei, dazu zwei themenrelevante Suchbegriffe (z. B. GELENKSCHMERZEN, ARTHROSE); keine Hashtags mehr

- Humor is capped at twice per month, and only warm and self-deprecating

- Newsletter style: no AI-sounding language, no em-dashes, no filler phrases, no emoji, no line breaks after every sentence — natural human writing

- Output defaults: copy-paste-ready, no screenplay/production notes in reel scripts, no unnecessary curation unless flagged transparently

- Bereitet ein Instagram-Serienformat (Reels-„Series“, Folge 1, 2 …) vor; Funktion bei ihr noch nicht freigeschaltet; Aufnahme soll so einfach wie möglich sein, am liebsten alle Folgen in einem Rutsch

Rhythm and scheduling

- Stories run out Fridays; Saturdays silent; Sundays restart the sequence — emotional/personal content first, then Abverkauf sequence

- Monday posts consistently underperform; avoid scheduling key content on Mondays

CTAs and offers in copy

- One CTA per post, always — no bifurcating CTAs and no double keyword triggers

- DARE TO B' RICHED is a serious coaching instrument, not a game; never the hook or focal point — mention only incidentally in CTAs

Claims and authority

- Claims authority comes from Aisun's own 20+ years of practice; no fabricated scientific authority

- Health claims follow "mutig aber sauber": personal healing story told fully, no universal cure promises, no instructions against medical treatment (especially for children)

- When addressing serious public events, use restraint and authenticity, avoid exploitative framing, and always note that energetic work complements rather than replaces professional support

Working notes

- Aisun often communicates via voice-to-text with transcription errors — interpret from context
